**Sixteen of the eighteen guerrilla stills are currently unused.** They are the strongest
remaining unplaced material: six carry a clean monument-against-sky separation, and five are
the magenta `SUCEDERÁ EN CDMX` poster/lona photographed in good light. The most narratively
honest homes, for the whole-film review to rule on:

- the poster stills (`STF00123`–`STF00134`) belong to the campaign-print track, not to a new
  beat — they are the printed artefact the film keeps referring to;
- the Torre Latinoamericana frames (`STF00152`, `STF00165`) are the one monument the film
  never shows in motion;
- `STF00008` / `STF00203` are the only frames where the figure faces a monument in daylight
  with a clean sky, which is the same separation Beat 9 already proves.

None of these should be added as a beat to use them up. They are listed so the whole-film
review can decide, and so no strong client material is lost by default.
