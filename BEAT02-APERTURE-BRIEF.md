# BEAT 2 — `SUCEDERÁ` APERTURE BRIEF

Prepared by Codex for the post-Beat-12 enhancement queue. This replaces the abandoned
“type behind the Ángel” idea for Beat 2.

## What is already working

`SUCEDERÁ` is the site’s signature moment: the city appears only through a variable-width
word on black. The current 11.58–12.48 source window was selected from measured motion inside
the glyphs, not from the whole frame. As the word becomes widest, the fill gains light and
movement rather than dying into dark/still buildings.

Do not sacrifice that for a more theatrical move.

## What this beat cannot do

The night source cannot support a clean monument depth split:

- the Ángel leaves the frame early in the usable passage;
- while present, it overlaps lit office façades at the same luminance;
- no colour key isolates it in the sodium-lit scene;
- compositing a daylight still into this night aerial would fabricate a scene.

No monument matte, hand rotoscoping, landmark foreground layer, fake light, or fabricated
parallax belongs here.

## The only permitted prototype

Test **aperture parallax inside the existing word**:

- the black field and word remain the composition;
- the word’s width mechanism remains dominant;
- move/scale the footage inside the glyphs subtly faster than the word’s outer geometry, so
  the city feels like it travels through an opening rather than simply playing behind text;
- keep the move primarily a controlled source crop/scale, not a 3D tilt or rotation;
- `IT WILL HAPPEN` stays quiet and fixed.

The move must be almost imperceptible in a still. In motion it should make widening equal
*exposure*: more of a moving city becomes available to the viewer.

## Hard limits

- Do not introduce a rectangular card, paper edge, border, or external contained frame around
  the word.
- Do not translate the entire black composition; the aperture is the word, not a floating UI
  object.
- Do not add an applied colour wash or use magenta.
- Do not shorten or re-cut the source window without rerunning the sampled-glyph luminance and
  motion measurements that selected it.
- Do not change the word’s final desktop/mobile fit.
- If the interior movement makes the letters less alive at the widest state, revert to the
  approved current version.

## Review evidence

Provide desktop and mobile states at narrow / widening / widest, plus forward, reverse, and
fast-scroll recordings. Also report the sampled region’s median luminance, p95 highlight, and
motion over the last third against the current baseline:

| Metric | Current floor / end |
|---|---|
| Median luminance | 75 → 89 |
| Highlights p95 | 184 → 239 |
| Motion, last third | 7.2 → 31.9 |

Approve only if the interior motion adds a perceptible aperture relationship without reducing
these values or competing with the width-axis signature. The fallback is the current Beat 2.
