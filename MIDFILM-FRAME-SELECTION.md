## Selected next prototype — civic flag pause

**Source:** `Other B-Roll/Screen Recording 2026-08-25 at 12.36.43 AM.mov`

**Clean window:** `2.6–5.9s` — Mexican flag against open sky, pedestrians and a cargo trike
crossing below, the Zócalo behind. The flagpole is a real vertical axis; foreground traffic gives
the plate life without requiring another fitness clip.

**Likely home:** the Act III threshold, after Beat 7's physical street occupation and before
Beat 8's handwritten qualifier message. It must be tested as a brief contained civic pause, not
added as a new full-screen beat.

**Sentence:** *A mark has been placed in the city; the city has a public scale; now the message
is delivered.*

**Choreography to test after the transition pass:**

1. The active street material releases into a tall/contained flag plate on black or a quiet
   campaign-print field—never a generic hard cut. Start with the dedicated
   `reference/authored-materials/v1/civic-flag-field-01.png`; its quiet centre is made for this
   ungraded portrait plate. `poster-edge-vertical-01.png` remains the quieter fallback.
2. The physical pole becomes the organizing vertical; the plate may rise or lock with a light
   registration edge, but the footage is never tinted, graded, or over-decorated.
3. Hold long enough to register sky, flag, and people crossing. No new slogan is required.
4. The contained plate gives way to Beat 8's real paper-message delivery. This must preserve
   Beat 8's stillness and complete Spanish message.

**Why this earns a place:** it introduces a second framed/editorial place in the film's hottest
middle stretch, reduces full-screen dominance where the audit says the reader is most convinced,
and brings civic Mexico into the website without pretending it is event footage.
