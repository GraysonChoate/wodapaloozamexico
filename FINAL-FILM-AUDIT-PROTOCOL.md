# FINAL FILM AUDIT PROTOCOL

Run after the approved enhancement queue is complete. This is a creative review, not a build
checklist. The question is not “do the scenes work?” It is:

> **Does the reader feel carried through one cinematic work, or does the page feel like
> individual well-edited clips changing on scroll?**

## Required evidence

Capture from a headed browser, not an embedded preview or headless browser:

1. One continuous normal-speed down-scroll, desktop 1440×900, from Beat 0 through the locked
   action index.
2. One continuous normal-speed down-scroll, mobile 375×812, across that same range.
3. A reverse scroll through every enhanced moment, including the action-index arrival.
4. A fast fling through every enhanced moment.
5. Named pause states at the start, middle, and release of every beat, plus the action-index
   black handoff, hairline, rise, lock, and final interactive state.
6. The existing no-JS and reduced-motion checks.

The continuous recordings are non-negotiable. Contact sheets tell us composition; only a
single uninterrupted pass can reveal whether rhythm, transition, and pressure accumulate.

## The four-act read

| Act | Beats | Required feeling |
|---|---|---|
| City / altitude | 0–3 | city at scale becomes descent; suspension, approach, identity entering the city |
| Descent | 4–6 | enclosure, arrival, street-level claim |
| Occupation | 7–9 | physical intervention, message, body becoming athlete |
| Proof / invitation | 10–13 | scale, legitimacy, city as venue, clear final action |

The pressure should tighten then release across these acts. A beat may be quiet, but no act
may feel like it merely waits for the next asset.

## Enhanced-moment checks

- **Beat 2:** widening type remains the hero; aperture movement is sensed, not displayed as
  an independent effect.
- **Beat 5:** planes → exact grid → centre dive reads as identity becoming athlete, not a
  photo gallery.
- **Beat 8:** contained portrait plate settles in sympathy with real paper; final date is
  untouched and still.
- **Beat 9:** foreground subject occludes secondary English copy; Spanish stays legible.
- **Beat 12:** photograph considered → full city replacement; never a card or crowd dissolve.

## Non-negotiable creative failures

Revise any moment that:

- reads as a scroll wipe, accidental crop, or generic page transition;
- uses a frame, texture, tilt, or parallax without a narrative reason;
- interrupts a message before it can be read;
- makes an enhanced move more obvious than the source footage;
- compromises source-native colour, source watermark framing, or factual copy;
- works only on downward scroll;
- repeats another beat’s signature move without changing its meaning.

## Final action and messaging audit

Before completion, verify every visible claim and destination against current project research
and operator-provided links. Required verified actions:

- Pre-Registro — exact operator URL required before launch.
- Inscríbete en el Online Qualifier — CompetitionCorner event 21707.
- Dudas y preguntas, aquí te ayudamos — supplied HubSpot form.
- Rulebook WZA CDMX — supplied Drive PDF.

Do not add generic SoCal language or unconfirmed operational information to fill final space.

The action index is not a footer. It must be judged as the final editorial place: no duplicate
film mark or active flight instrument over the locked print surface; all destinations legible,
obvious, and reachable; and return-to-top remains present at the final pixel. The English-led
heading / Spanish-action relationship is a whole-sequence copy decision, not a local styling fix.

## Decision rule

The finished piece does not need an effect in every beat. It needs a small set of excellent
moves that recur as a coherent language: aperture, frame, plane, body, hard replacement,
black release. If a new addition cannot improve that language, leave the validated film alone.
