# WZA MEXICO CITY — STORYBOARD v3

**A short film navigated by scrolling. Taken to eleven.**

v3 supersedes v2. Decisions locked: **English-led** with Spanish only where it hits hardest.
**Silent.** **No venue named** — the city is the venue and the film already argued it.

This is a mockup whose job is to be the most cinematic thing anyone in that room has seen.
Not a production site. So: no ticket flow, no real links, no hedging. Every constraint I was
respecting for production has been dropped in favour of impact.

---

## THE FIVE RULES

1. **Cinematic framing** — every section is a composed shot.
2. **Descent and pressure** — wide and cold at altitude, dense and loud at street level.
3. **Gradient and ground** — `#6CC180 → #35A7DF` is the signal. Black is the ground. The
   city's own walls supply every other colour.
4. **Restrained compositing** — one dominant image · one supporting texture · one graphic
   action. Restraint is what makes the loud moments land.
5. **The rhythm** — motion → stillness → detail → tension → motion.

---

## THREE THINGS THAT RUN THE WHOLE PAGE

These are the devices that make it feel like one film rather than thirteen sections.

### 1 · THE ALTIMETER
A persistent readout, top right, monospace, tiny. It counts **down** as you scroll:

    2,240 m ────────────── 0 m

It starts at Mexico City's altitude and lands at street level on the competition floor. It
is the descent made literal, it is on screen the entire time, and it costs almost nothing.
At `0 m` it stops being a number and becomes the word **FLOOR**.

### 2 · THE WIDTH AXIS
Normalidad has a width axis. Every display word on the page is bound to scroll position:
**Compact at 2,240 m, UltraExtended at 0 m.** Type physically opens as you descend. Most
brands cannot do this. Theirs can, and nobody has used it.

### 3 · THE GRADIENT AS A LIGHT SOURCE
`#6CC180 → #35A7DF` is not a fill. It is a light that travels — it rakes across type, edges
a frame, bleeds from behind a monument, lights the underside of a mask. It appears when
Mexico City is speaking and disappears when Miami is.

# ACT I · ALTITUDE
*Cold. Wide. Silent. Type at its narrowest.*

### BEAT 1 — APPROACH
**Image** film `0:05–0:06` — the cockpit. Instruments, windscreen, the city below.
**Action** the shot holds. Two full seconds of nothing but the aircraft.
**Type** appears letter by letter, Compact, tiny:

> CIUDAD DE MÉXICO · 2,240 m

**To eleven** — the altimeter initialises here with a fast count-up to 2,240 and then locks.
The site announces its own mechanic in the first three seconds.

### BEAT 2 — THE CITY TAKES THE FRAME
**Image** film `0:02–0:04` — aerial, the city running to the mountains
**Action** slow push in.
**Type**

> **SUCEDERÁ**
> IT WILL HAPPEN

**To eleven** — `SUCEDERÁ` is **masked by the footage itself.** The letterforms are a
window onto the moving aerial; black everywhere else. The city is literally inside the word.
As you scroll, the type widens and more city becomes visible through it.

*This is the single best use of their brand on this page and it should be the shot people
remember.*

### BEAT 3 — THE MARK
**Image** black.
**Action** the badge **draws itself** — SVG path animation, the shield stroking on in the
gradient, then the banner, then the script.
**Type** the mark alone.
**To eleven** — the drawn stroke is the gradient, so the mark appears to be *lit into
existence* rather than faded in. Hold three full seconds after it completes. Absolute
silence and stillness. This is the pause that buys everything after it.

# ACT II · DESCENT
*Out of the sky, into a car, onto the street.*

### BEAT 4 — GROUND TRANSPORT
**Image** film `0:07–0:09` — car interior at night → the CDMX sign
**Action** a 2.39:1 letterbox that **opens vertically** to full bleed as you scroll. A
shutter opening. The altimeter is falling fast now.
**Type**

> The standard of Miami. Now in México.

**To eleven** — headlight streaks from the footage bleed *outside* the video frame into the
page margins, so the light escapes its container.

### BEAT 5 — THE IDENTITY LOOP
**Image** `SUD_01–09` + the 18 stills
**Action** the spine of the site:

    one figure, from behind, motionless
    → the monument changes behind them
    → the badge alternates WHITE (01–05, 09) and CYAN (06, 08)
    → the crop tightens with each step
    → frames begin to overlap and register like a contact sheet
    → the sheet ASSEMBLES into a grid of nine
    → then all nine cut to ONE full frame
    → MATCH CUT to film 0:29 — an athlete entering the same raised-arm pose

**Type** none. The badge on the hoodie is the type.
**To eleven** — the clips are locked-off long takes: **the figure holds absolutely still
while cars, a red bus and crowds stream past.** Play them at slightly different rates so
the nine panels breathe out of sync. The person never moves. The city never stops. Then
they all collapse into one and become an athlete.

*The body is individual. The mark is collective. That is the whole event in one beat.*

### BEAT 6 — THE BANNER
**Image** `LONA00130198.mov` — the banner unfurling down Monumento a la Revolución
**Action** **scroll-driven unfurl.** You scroll, it drops. The one place where scroll IS
the mechanic, because the real-world action already was one.
**Type** carried on the banner — *SUCEDERÁ EN CDMX*. Nothing added.
**To eleven** — the banner does not stop at the bottom of the monument. **It keeps
unrolling past the edge of the viewport and becomes the ground of the next section.** The
page is now standing on the banner.

# ACT III · STREET
*Ground level. Dense, warm, loud. Type near its widest.*

### BEAT 7 — OCCUPATION
**Image** `STICKERS_03_V1.mov` — the badge on a teal post, a yellow wall, a purple mural
**Action** a horizontal filmstrip travelling sideways as the page scrolls down
**Type** frame-edge labels, camera-metadata register:

> REFORMA · 06:41 · NO PERMIT · NO CUTS

**To eleven** — as each sticker enters frame, the page's own accent **momentarily takes
that wall's colour.** Yellow wall, the labels go yellow. Purple mural, they go purple. The
city is colour-grading the interface. **Do not tint the footage — invert the relationship.**

### BEAT 8 — THE MESSAGE
**Image** `QUALIFIER_v3_05.mov` — the figure holding the newspaper in the street
**Action** hold. Almost nothing moves. The altimeter is nearly at zero.
**Type** the newspaper carries it:

> THE ONLINE QUALIFIER
> AUGUST 18–26

**To eleven** — do the opposite of everything before it. **Kill the gradient. Kill the
grain. Kill the motion.** One still frame, one line of type, five full seconds of scroll
where almost nothing happens. Silence before the door opens.

### BEAT 9 — EARN IT
**Image** a still from the guerrilla set — the hooded figure, back to camera, **arms raised
in the flex** — held almost black, lit only by gradient raking in from the left edge.
**Texture** halftone extracted from the guerrilla banner
**Action** the type sets over the figure at Normalidad **UltraExtended**. The width axis,
travelling since Beat 2, arrives here. Then as the scroll completes, **the type drops away
and leaves the figure alone in frame** — still, arms up, holding.
**Type**

> **Es momento de**
> **ganarte tu lugar.**

> It's time to earn your place.

**To eleven** — the one moment Spanish leads and English follows beneath in small type. It
is their own line, it is the thesis of the event, and it is the largest type on the page.

**THE ACT III → IV MATCH CUT.** Beat 9 ends on the anonymous figure, arms raised, dead
still. Beat 10 opens on film `0:29` — **an athlete entering the identical pose.** Same
silhouette, same frame position, cut on the shape. The nobody in the hoodie becomes a
competitor on the floor. That is the entire promise of the event executed in one cut, and
it costs no new footage.

*Two match cuts, deliberately paired: Beat 5 turns nine figures into one; Beat 9 turns that
one figure into an athlete. Same device, twice, escalating.*

# ACT IV · THE FLOOR
*Impact. The altimeter reads 0 m and becomes FLOOR.*

### BEAT 10 — WHAT THIS IS
**Image** film `0:29–0:33` — the giant WZA letters with athletes running through, into the
competition floor
**Action** **match cut on the raised-arm pose** from Beat 9 — the hooded figure becomes an
athlete without the frame moving. Then the floor erupts. After Beat 8's stillness this is a
door opening.
**Type**

> CELEBRATE FITNESS. COMMUNITY. LIFE.

**To eleven** — the one place magenta is permitted. This is WZA-global speaking, and the
temperature shift from cool to hot IS the message: the standard arriving. Everything before
was green-blue. This one section runs Miami.

### BEAT 11 — THE PROOF
**Image** film `0:36–0:37` — the celebrating athlete, then the screaming face
**Action** slow push on a single face until it fills the frame
**Type**

> ATHLETES (2,000+) · DIVISIONS (16) · FIRST EDITION (1)

**To eleven** — the numbers **count up** as they enter, then the last one lands on `1` and
stops dead. There is only one first edition. Ever.

### BEAT 12 — THE CITY IS THE VENUE
**Image** film `0:46` the Zócalo empty → `0:55` the Zócalo packed at night
**Action** **the same frame, twice.** Empty, then — on a single scroll gesture — full.
**Type**

> **DECEMBER 4 · 5 · 6 · 2026**
> Ciudad de México

**To eleven** — the empty Zócalo has already appeared earlier in the scroll as a quiet
establishing shot. Here it returns and **fills with 40,000 people in one move.** The
audience remembers the empty square. That memory is what makes the full one land.

*This is how we answer the venue question without naming a venue: the largest square in
Latin America, empty and then full.*

### BEAT 13 — CLOSE
**Image** film `0:56–0:58` — Monumento a la Revolución **lit red**, the badge landing
**Action** the badge locks exactly as the film ends
**Type** the mark, then the links block on black
**To eleven** — as the badge lands, the **gradient bleeds out from behind the monument** and
floods the frame edge-to-edge for a beat, then drains to black. Then the altimeter, which
has been running the whole page, resolves:

    FLOOR

## THE MONUMENT COLOUR CUE

The film contains El Ángel **lit brand-green** at `0:12` and Revolución **lit red** at
`0:56`. That is the city already wearing the palette. Use both, and use them as act
markers: green opens the descent, red closes the floor.

## WHAT WE ARE NOT DOING

- **No audio.** Silent, per the decision. Every beat above works without it.
- **No venue named.** The Zócalo device answers it.
- **No stock or outside footage.** Different uniforms and lighting break the world.
- **No fiesta iconography.** No papel picado, marigolds or calaveras.
- **No magenta**, except Beat 10, deliberately.

## CORRECTIONS FROM THE BUILD

Written down because each one was wrong in a way that reads as right until you check.

**The badge alternation is WHITE 01–05 and 09, CYAN 06 and 08.** v3 listed 09 as cyan. It is
white — confirmed by cropping the badge on the mid-frame of every clip and looking at it at
size. Two automated attempts to classify it by colour both failed, locking onto bright sky
rather than the artwork, and both returned a confident WHITE for all eight including the two
that are plainly cyan. The eye on a tight crop settled it.

**There is no SUD_07.** The set is eight clips, not nine. The ninth panel of the contact
sheet is a photograph — `STF00176` — which is also the only frame in the beat that carries
the Torre Latinoamericana, and it carries a cyan badge. So the still is not filler: it adds
a monument and reinforces the alternation. It also answers the note that this beat has to
prove the still/video relationship, since it is the one panel that never moves.

**The match cut is at 31.07–31.61, not 0:29.** There is no athlete at 0:29 — that window is
the Miami beach and an aerial stadium, both carrying burned-in `FROM DECEMBER 4th TO 6th`
titles. The pose arrives at 31.07: an adaptive athlete pressing a barbell from chin to full
overhead lockout. It is the better cut on its own terms, because the figure already HOLDS
the raised-arm pose and the athlete ARRIVES at it — which is what the beat asked for. The
film burns its own mark into the bottom-right of this shot, so the encode crops the right
15% rather than setting that mark opposite ours in the same frame.

**A match cut cannot sit on a section boundary.** Built as its own section it arrived as a
wipe — a sticky stage releases and slides, and between a dark street and a white tent that
edge is unmissable. The lift now lives inside Beat 5 and switches on at a single scroll
position, after the dive to one full frame has completed. Nine raised arms, then one.

**The panels play; they do not scrub.** The figure is motionless while the city streams past,
so looping at uneven rates (0.78x to 1.18x) is what makes the nine breathe out of sync.
Scrubbing eight all-keyframe clips at once would also have cost 36 MB against 4.3 MB.
