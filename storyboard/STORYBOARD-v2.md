# WZA MEXICO CITY — STORYBOARD v2

**A short film navigated by scrolling.**

v2 supersedes v1. Written after viewing **every** asset: all 58 seconds of the film at one
frame per second, all 8 Sudadera clips, all 18 stills, all four logo lockups, the ARTES
production artwork, and the hex values written inside the usage guide.

---

## THE FIVE RULES

1. **Cinematic framing** — every section is a composed shot, not a container.
2. **Descent and pressure** — quiet and wide at altitude, dense and loud at street level.
   Type widens, crops tighten, overlays multiply *as we go down*.
3. **Gradient and ground** — `#6CC180` → `#35A7DF` is the signal. Black is the ground. The
   city's own walls supply every other colour.
4. **Restrained compositing** — per frame: one dominant image · one supporting texture ·
   one graphic action. Three is the ceiling.
5. **The rhythm** — motion → stillness → detail → tension → motion.

**Every layer must:** establish place · increase pressure · clarify identity · create a
pause. A layer doing none of them gets cut.

---

## WHAT CHANGED FROM v1

- **The film opens in an aeroplane cockpit** (0:05–0:06). Missed at 4-second sampling.
  The descent is literal: flight → drive → street → floor. This is now Beat 1.
- **Beat 5 is rebuilt as the identity loop**, not parallax panels.
- **Official hexes corrected** — `#6CC180` / `#35A7DF`, written inside the usage guide by
  the designer. Earlier sampled values were desaturated.
- **The Sudadera clips are locked-off long takes** where the figure is motionless and the
  city moves around them. That changes how Beat 5 and 7 work.
- New shots found: El Ángel lit brand-green, Xochimilco trajineras, the Autódromo aerial,
  a giant WZA letter installation, Bellas Artes, a metro train, and Monumento a la
  Revolución lit red at the close.

# ACT I · ALTITUDE
*Cold, wide, almost silent. Type at its narrowest.*

### BEAT 1 — APPROACH
**Image** film `0:05–0:06` — the cockpit. Instruments, the windscreen, the city below.
**Texture** none.
**Action** the footage runs. Nothing else moves.
**Type** after two seconds of nothing, small, Normalidad **Compact**:

> CIUDAD DE MÉXICO · 2,240 m

**Job** establish place · create a pause
**Why this and not the aerial** — the cockpit puts the viewer *inside* the arrival instead
of above it. It is the first-person frame the whole site is built on, and the film hands
it to us in its own opening.

### BEAT 2 — THE CITY TAKES THE FRAME
**Image** film `0:02–0:04` — the aerial, city running to the mountains
**Texture** grain harvested from the guerrilla stills
**Action** one slow push in.
**Type** the width axis states itself. `SUCEDERÁ` sets Compact and **widens toward Extended**
as the push completes.

> **SUCEDERÁ**
> EN CDMX

**Job** clarify identity
**Note** width = pressure. It widens again in every act and arrives in Beat 9.

### BEAT 3 — THE MARK
**Image** black.
**Texture** none.
**Action** the badge draws in the `#6CC180 → #35A7DF` gradient, then holds.
**Type** the mark alone.
**Job** create a pause

# ACT II · DESCENT
*Out of the sky, into a car, onto the street. Type opens.*

### BEAT 4 — GROUND TRANSPORT
**Image** film `0:07–0:09` — car interior at night, then the CDMX sign
**Texture** windscreen light streaks, native
**Action** letterboxed 2.39:1 that **opens vertically** to full bleed. A shutter opening.
**Type** bottom left, subtitle register:

> El estándar de Miami ahora en México.

**Job** establish place

### BEAT 5 — THE IDENTITY LOOP
**Image** `Sudadera/SUD_01–09` + the 18 stills
**Texture** none. This photography needs nothing on it.
**Action** the spine of the whole site:

    one figure, from behind, almost still
    → the monument changes behind them
    → the badge alternates WHITE (01–05) and CYAN (06/08/09)
    → the crop tightens with each step
    → images begin to overlap and register like a contact sheet
    → resolves into one full frame
    → MATCH CUT into film 0:19 or 0:29 — an athlete entering the same raised-arm pose

**Type** none. The badge on the hoodie is the type.
**Job** clarify identity
**Why it works** — the clips are locked-off long takes: the figure holds the flex while
cars, a red bus and crowds move past. **The stillness is in the subject and the motion is
in the city.** That is the argument of the whole event in one shot, and it means we can
scrub these clips without fighting a camera move.
**The match cut is the transition into Act IV material.** No new footage required.

### BEAT 6 — THE BANNER
**Image** `lona 1/LONA00130198.mov` — the operator's pick. The banner unfurling down the
wall of Monumento a la Revolución.
**Texture** the vinyl's own crease and halftone
**Action** the unfurl is **scroll-driven**. You scroll, it drops. The one beat where scroll
IS the mechanic, because the real-world action already was one.
**Type** carried on the banner — *SUCEDERÁ EN CDMX*. Nothing added.
**Job** increase pressure
**Note** give it a full screen and do not decorate it.

## SHOTS THE CLIENT SHOULD CAPTURE

Not to make the site busier — to supply connective tissue we genuinely lack:

- close-ups: hands, grips, chalk, shoes, tape, a barbell knurl
- quiet portraits and recovery moments
- clean background plates for type and transitions
- environmental CDMX detail at street level
- apparel and sticker product detail
- **anything with the athlete's face** — the guerrilla figure is deliberately anonymous,
  and Act IV currently leans entirely on Miami footage for human faces

**No outside or stock competition footage.** Different uniforms, venues and lighting break
the world, and this campaign's whole power is that it is real.

## STILL OPEN

- **Venue** — not public. Structural.
- **Language** — this draft is Spanish-led with English at the global moments. Needs a
  decision; it changes the type metrics.
- **Audio** — the film has a track, the clips carry 24-bit PCM. A silent cinematic site is
  a contradiction; autoplay audio is hostile. Needs a decision.
- **Ticketing / registration flow** — if one exists it changes Act IV.
- **Mobile** — the vertical clips will be *better* on mobile than desktop. Design for that
  deliberately rather than inheriting it.
- **`logo_WZA.ai` and `logo_asdeporte.ai`** are white-on-transparent and cannot be rendered
  without Illustrator. Confirmed as white versions of marks already documented — no
  information is missing, but they are unopened.
