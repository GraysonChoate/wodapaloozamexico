# WZA MEXICO CITY — STORYBOARD v1

**A short film navigated by scrolling.**

Every beat names a real file. Nothing here assumes a shot we do not have. Where a beat
needs something we lack, it says so out loud.

---

## THE FOUR RULES

1. **Cinematic framing** — every section is a composed shot, not a container.
2. **Descent and pressure** — quiet and wide at altitude, dense and loud at street level.
   Type widens, crops tighten, overlays multiply, motion accelerates *as we go down*.
3. **Gradient and ground** — green→blue `#85c18a → #5298c8` is the signal. Black is the
   ground. The city's own walls supply every other colour.
4. **Restrained compositing** — per frame: **one dominant image · one supporting texture ·
   one graphic action.** Count them. Three is the ceiling.

**Every layer must do one of four jobs:** establish place · increase pressure · clarify
identity · create a pause. A layer doing none of them gets cut.

Reforma actually sees.

# ACT I · ALTITUDE
*Cold. Wide. Almost silent. Type at its narrowest.*

### BEAT 1 — COLD OPEN
**Image** `WZA_MX_LOGO.mp4` 00:00–00:04 — the aerial, city running to the mountains
**Texture** none. The frame is clean.
**Action** the film plays. Nothing else moves.
**Type** nothing for the first two seconds. Then, small, Normalidad **Compact**, centred low:

> CIUDAD DE MÉXICO · 2,240 m

**Job** establish place
**Note** hold the silence. The whole descent depends on starting from stillness. This is
the pause that buys us the noise later.

### BEAT 2 — THE GRID
**Image** `WZA_MX_LOGO.mp4` 00:12–00:16 — straight-down aerial, the endless grid
**Texture** film grain harvested from the guerrilla stills
**Action** the frame slowly pushes in. One move only.
**Type** the first width shift. `SUCEDERÁ` sets in Compact and **widens toward Extended**
as the push completes.

> **SUCEDERÁ**
> EN CDMX

**Job** clarify identity
**Note** this is the signature typographic move stated early so the reader learns the
language. Width = pressure. It will widen further every act.

### BEAT 3 — THE MARK, HELD
**Image** black. No footage.
**Texture** none.
**Action** the badge draws itself in the green→blue gradient, then holds.
**Type** the mark only.
**Job** create a pause
**Note** a full screen of black this early is a risk and it is the right one. The
references earn their impact through restraint before it.

# ACT II · DESCENT
*We drop out of the sky and into a car. Cool becomes warm. Type opens.*

### BEAT 4 — ARRIVAL
**Image** `WZA_MX_LOGO.mp4` 00:04–00:08 — night interior, POV from inside the car
**Texture** windscreen light streaks (native to the footage)
**Action** the footage runs; the frame is letterboxed to a 2.39:1 crop that **opens
vertically** to full-bleed as we scroll. A shutter opening.
**Type** bottom left, small, editorial:

> El estándar de Miami ahora en México.

**Job** establish place
**Note** first appearance of Spanish. It should feel like a subtitle, not a headline.

### BEAT 5 — THE FIGURE
**Image** `Sudadera/` vertical clips — the hooded figure at El Ángel, back to camera
**Texture** none. Photography this good needs nothing on it.
**Action** three vertical clips side by side, each scrubbing at a slightly different rate
as the page scrolls. Parallax between panels, not within them.
**Type** none in the frame. The badge on the hoodie *is* the type.
**Job** clarify identity
**Note** this is where vertical becomes an asset. Three tall panels = three monuments,
one figure, and the shape is native to the footage.

### BEAT 6 — THE BANNER
**Image** `lona 1/LONA*.mov` — the banner unfurling down the wall of Monumento a la
Revolución
**Texture** the vinyl's own crease and halftone
**Action** the unfurl is **scroll-driven**. The banner drops as you scroll. This is the one
beat where scroll IS the mechanic, because the real-world action already is one.
**Type** carried on the banner itself — *SUCEDERÁ EN CDMX* — no added type.
**Job** increase pressure
**Note** the strongest single asset in the package. Give it a whole screen and do not
decorate it.

## WHAT THIS STORYBOARD IS MISSING

Stated plainly rather than papered over:

- **No venue.** Structural, not fixable by design.
- **No ticket or registration flow.** If one exists it changes Act IV.
- **Language not settled.** This draft is Spanish-led with English at the global moments.
  It needs a decision, and it changes the type metrics.
- **The 8 Sudadera clips are unviewed** — I specced all 13 vertical clips but only
  frame-checked 3. Beat 5 assumes they are usable; that needs confirming.
- **No audio plan.** The film has a track. Thirteen clips have 24-bit PCM. A muted
  cinematic site is a contradiction, but autoplay audio is hostile. Needs a decision.
- **No mobile pass.** Vertical clips will be *better* on mobile than desktop, which is
  unusual and worth designing for deliberately rather than inheriting.
