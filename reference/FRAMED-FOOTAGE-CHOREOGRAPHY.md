# Framed Footage Choreography

## Purpose

Use campaign-print material to turn selected footage fragments into **moving editorial objects**.
This expands what qualifies for the site: a shot need not fill a viewport to be valuable. It can
be a crop, a detail, a human-scale window, or a moving piece of city texture with a specific job.

## The repeatable sentence

**Print ground arrives → framed footage fragment crosses/locks → reader gets a pause → frame
either expands into the world or yields to the next object.**

The graphic material is page-owned. Footage remains its own untouched plane within the frame.

## Four allowed moves

1. **Street-window entrance** — a crop window slides in from outside the field, accompanied by
   a restrained ink/halftone edge. It stops long enough for the image or copy to register.
2. **Scale-through** — the same window enlarges until its crop becomes the next full-bleed shot.
   This is only for a source that can survive enlargement without losing its point.
3. **Plane field to one frame** — two to four related city/athlete crops enter at authored shallow
   depths, align to a single plane, then one is selected as the next image. Never a generic
   carousel; each crop must establish a real scale or relationship.
4. **Poster pause** — an authored print field holds a physical moving plate or sparse fact/action.
   It is a real web place: scroll slows, copy is readable, links can exist.

## Material rules

- Use `authored-materials/v1/print-field-01.png` or later siblings as the print ground.
- Cyan, magenta, yellow and black may belong to the campaign-print object; persistent UI remains
  green → blue / black / white.
- Ink, halftone, overspray and registration may enter with a frame or exit with it; they must not
  stain, recolour or pretend to be light inside client footage.
- No rounded generic cards, stock-paper UI, or decorative texture on every beat.

## Candidate source types

- day aerial / traffic details: city as an object, not a wallpaper background;
- pedestrian/cyclist footage: human scale before athletic scale;
- a clean athlete movement fragment: a crop whose motion can carry a copy line;
- car/cockpit detail: an interior window that can step into a larger world;
- client footage with burned-in copy: only if a crop isolates a meaningful usable region without
  clipping an official watermark or inventing a new reading.

## Placement test

Before adding a framed fragment, name all three:

1. What specific information or emotional change does the crop reveal?
2. Why is it contained here instead of full bleed?
3. Where does the frame go next: lock, expand, collide, or release?

If those answers are not concrete, keep the existing film beat unchanged.

## First integration target

Do not spread this across the film yet. In the next pass, prototype one **city-to-human-scale
editorial interruption** using cleared daytime overhead/foot-traffic source if supplied; until
then, use the current material only to define the grammar. Review it inside the full sequence
before adding any second instance.
