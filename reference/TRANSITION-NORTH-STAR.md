# TRANSITION NORTH STAR

Three reference recordings, watched frame by frame. This is the standard every seam
in the film is measured against. Sources live in `Better Transitions/` (not committed
— client-supplied screen recordings).

The tooling in them is irrelevant. Higgsfield is a way of producing two of these; none
of the devices below need it. What matters is the grammar.

---

## 1 · KINETIC TYPOGRAPHY REEL — 25.4s

**One word at a time, and the sentence builds.** `HELLO` → `HELLO AND` → `HELLO AND
WELCOME` → `HELLO AND WELCOME TO`. Words accumulate in place rather than replacing
each other, and the composition reflows around each new arrival.

**Wild scale contrast inside one frame.** `CREATE YOUR OWN` sits tiny beside `VIDEO`
at five times the size. Hierarchy comes from scale, not from position.

**Words rotate.** `OWN` and `SALES` stand vertically against the horizontal lines.
Ninety degrees is a legitimate move, not an accident.

**Type is a window onto footage.** `KINETIC`, `TO`, `MORE`, `QUALITY`, `TIME` are all
filled with city plates. This is the device already built into Beat 8 → 9.

**Footage almost never runs full bleed.** It arrives inside a horizontal letterbox
band, and the band's height is animated — `PROMOTE YOUR PRODUCT / GET MORE CUSTOMERS`
sits on a strip a fifth of the frame tall, then `ORDER NOW` opens it wider. Small
frames are the norm; the full frame is the exception, held for impact.

## 2 · INK REVEAL — 6.0s

A single organic device: ink drips from the top edge, falls, hits, and **splatters
into the shape that becomes the logo's ground**. One liquid mask, no cuts, no slide.
The frame is never divided — it is *stained* into the next state.

## 3 · HIGGSFIELD EDIT — 25.2s

**The circular collapse.** This is the one he named. A full-frame shot does not cut
and does not slide: it **collapses into a circle**, the circle shrinks and travels to
the bottom-right, and the incoming scene is uncovered in the space it vacates. The
circle then stays as a picture-in-picture and the film continues around it.

**Type passes BEHIND the subject.** `TRANSITION`, `BUT WHAT IF`, `wall` all run behind
the person while he stays in front. Beat 9 already owns this via the subject matte.

**Two voices in one line.** Bold condensed sans against an italic serif — `I told
you`, `easy as`, `just straight sauce`, `no fluff`. The contrast is the whole design.

**Type is anchored, not centred.** Bottom-left, mid-left, low-right. It sits in the
scene rather than on top of it.

**Chromatic split and whip-blur** carry a few of the cuts — RGB fringing, then a
smear, then the new shot.

---

## THE RULES THIS PRODUCES

1. **Nothing slides.** A scene collapses, is stained over, is cut through, or is
   uncovered. It never travels up out of frame. (Already law: see the z-index chain.)
2. **A shape carries the seam** — a letterform, a band, a circle, a splatter. The
   shape comes from the image, not from a library.
3. **What is inside the shape is the next scene**, at its own crop and its own scale.
4. **Type is a material.** It masks, it occludes, it stands vertically, it changes
   size by a factor of five inside one frame.
5. **Bands and panels beat full bleed.** A smaller frame is more cinematic than a
   bigger one, and it unlocks footage that cannot survive being held full-frame.
