# Campaign Material Map — Source-First

**Purpose:** turn the Mexico campaign’s physical, printed world into a small reusable
editorial-material kit without fabricating a graphic style or grading client footage.

This is a live source inventory. It does not authorize blanket texture application.

## The confirmed visual language

The real guerrilla poster establishes a much richer language than the current film alone:

- black / charcoal printed ground;
- cyan-to-light-cyan brush or paint shape carrying `SUCEDERÁ EN CDMX`;
- hot-pink halftone-dot fields and magenta registration accents;
- white/light logo ink with cyan sign geometry;
- physical folds, creases, grommets, and edge distortion;
- dark volcanic-looking masonry against pale limestone architecture;
- a real human installing/occupying the campaign object.

It is important that this reads as **campaign print installed in Mexico City**, not as a
generic distressed-overlay aesthetic.

## Critical asset gap

The supplied screenshot of the large Mexico City composite artwork (paper ground, landmark
cutouts, cyan/magenta/yellow print treatment, large black Wodapalooza sign) is a **reference
for the campaign world**, but its clean high-resolution master is not present in this local
asset set.

Do not recreate that poster from the screenshot. Request or locate the original artwork in
Drive before using its landmark cutouts, coloured brush fields, or full sign composition as
editable graphic assets.

Until that master is available, use the real photographed poster for physical evidence and
the supplied vector mark for clean identity—not a fake reconstruction.

## Candidate editorial uses — deliberately limited

1. **Beat 7 / street threshold:** a source-derived sticker or poster-contact detail can
   make the passage into the street world feel physically installed, not digitally decorated.
2. **Beat 8:** retain the actual paper performance; at most, use a related poster edge or
   print fragment as an adjacent chapter threshold, never on top of the cue cards.
3. **One chapter orientation moment:** a contained guerrilla-poster photograph may hold a
   short factual line or section label, then release back into footage.
4. **Final action index:** use official vector marks and a campaign-derived material boundary
   to make actions feel like an editorial object. It must not become a generic rounded card.

## Guardrails

- No all-page paper or halftone layer.
- No generic CSS magenta applied to the interface.
- Do not colour-grade video toward campaign swatches.
- Do not lift distorted poster copy as if it were flat typography.
- Do not use a texture crop merely because it looks cool; it must create a narrative pause,
  physical threshold, or action destination.
- If the real high-resolution campaign master arrives, update this map before producing any
  extracted assets.
