## Selection rule

For any new framed item, answer before implementation:

1. What does this fragment reveal that a full screen would conceal?
2. What physical page material explains its boundary?
3. Does it lock, expand, align, or release—and why at that point in the story?

If the answer is simply “it looks cool,” it remains in the ledger.
