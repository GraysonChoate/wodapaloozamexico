# FUNCTIONAL WEB LAYER — POST-FILM SPEC

**Tracks:** official event/action system; typography/copy; whole-film pacing and QA.

The site must remain a cinematic scroll experience, but it also has to let a visitor orient,
act, and leave with useful information. This is a *utility layer*, not a conventional marketing
navigation bar. It is intentionally scheduled after the transition pass and the civic-flag proof,
so it can respond to the real rhythm of the finished film.

## Recommended system

### 1. Persistent, minimal top rail

- Left: compact Wodapalooza Mexico City mark / home control.
- Centre: a quiet position readout, for example `01 / 14`, that changes only at designed places
  rather than pretending every scroll pixel is a menu.
- Right: one primary action, `QUALIFIER ↗`, linked to the verified CompetitionCorner URL.

It is transparent over footage and uses the existing neutral ground only where contrast requires
it. It should enter with the cockpit/HUD world, disappear over the action index, and never become
a large opaque header.

### 2. A deliberate section index, not a permanent menu

The mark may open a small editorial index on black: `THE CITY`, `QUALIFIER`, `THE PROOF`,
`DEC 4–6`, `ENTER`. Each item scrolls to an actual held place. It must not promise sections that
do not exist or force viewers out of the film.

This draws the useful lesson from the SoCal site: clear pathways and official hierarchy. It does
not borrow its portal look, sponsor clutter, or conventional event-site layout.

### 3. Action ownership

- The ending action index is the destination for all actions and informational links.
- The persistent `QUALIFIER ↗` is an escape hatch for high-intent visitors; it should not compete
  with every beat's copy.
- Pre-Registro remains visibly unavailable and unfocusable until the complete official URL is
  supplied.
- Instagram and Asdeporte remain supporting utilities at the ending, not repeated floating CTAs.

### 4. Mobile behavior

On mobile, retain the mark and a single `QUALIFIER ↗` control; open the index from the mark. The
position readout may collapse to a thin progress rule. Controls must remain reachable without
obscuring the Spanish paper/message beats.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every persistent control has a real destination or an explicit unavailable state.
- The rail never covers a face, the wide `SUCEDERÁ` aperture, or a held message.
- It is keyboard usable, readable on actual footage, and hidden/released before the final print
  action index locks.
- It improves orientation and conversion without flattening the film into a standard homepage.

## Build order

1. Approve the transition pass.
2. Prototype and audit the civic-flag interruption.
3. Perform the next whole-film review.
4. Build this utility layer against the resulting locked scroll map.
